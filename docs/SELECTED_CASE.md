## Case A — Stable but epistemically fragile

**Signal:** `NM-OBS-001`

The observation reports high stability with a non-zero factual fragility signal. OGS permits internal observation and a measurement note, but prohibits public claims, recommendations, execution triggers, and customer-facing statements.

**Outcome:** bounded `limited_use`; no external action.
