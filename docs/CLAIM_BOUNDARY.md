# Public Claim Boundary

## Permitted

> Five synthetic NeoMundi-style observation payloads were processed through an explicit OGS admissibility mapping. Five corrected payload-bound receipts were generated, and internal deterministic replay reproduced all five documented classifications under the same payloads and explicit rules. No external action was authorized.

## Required qualification

> Synthetic, internally verified, not independently reproduced, and not production-derived.

## Prohibited

Do not claim:

- production integration;
- real-world safety;
- regulatory approval or certification;
- live customer data;
- autonomous remediation;
- independent reproduction;
- malicious-insider-resistant receipts;
- that NeoMundi measurements are inherently true;
- that OGS validates the underlying measurement methodology.
