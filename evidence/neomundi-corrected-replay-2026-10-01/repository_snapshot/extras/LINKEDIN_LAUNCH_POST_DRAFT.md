**Measurement is not authority.**

AI systems increasingly produce runtime telemetry, anomaly scores, risk indicators, and drift signals. But an observation does not determine what an institution is permitted to do with it.

NeoMundi and Operational Governance Systems / Project Petunia × Judicator have developed a synthetic interoperability proof of method around that boundary:

```text
runtime observation
→ structured signal
→ admissibility evaluation
→ bounded reliance / review / refusal / no-bind
→ receipt
→ replay
```

The controlled result covers five synthetic NeoMundi-style payloads, five corrected payload-bound receipts, and five successful internal deterministic replays under the same payloads and explicit rules. No external action was authorized.

The result is intentionally bounded: synthetic, internally verified, not independently reproduced, and not production-derived.

The governing principle is simple:

**NeoMundi observes. OGS adjudicates what the observation may become.**

The versioned public repository contains the architecture, public-safe examples, evidence classification, limitations, claim boundary, receipts, replay structure, and integrity verifier.
