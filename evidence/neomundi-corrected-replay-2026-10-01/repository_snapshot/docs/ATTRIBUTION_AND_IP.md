# Attribution and Intellectual-Property Boundary

## Recommended attribution

> Joint public interoperability demonstration by NeoMundi and Operational Governance Systems / Project Petunia × Judicator.

This wording remains subject to final joint approval.

## Mark Mocnaj / OGS

Mark Mocnaj retains ownership of pre-existing OGS and Project Petunia × Judicator materials, including public and private architecture, terminology, admissibility logic, governance-object design, receipt interpretation, replay and claim-boundary language, documentation, and visual identity.

## NeoMundi

NeoMundi retains ownership of its pre-existing architecture, terminology, runtime-signal definitions, source code, implementation details, branding, marks, public technical claims, and commercialization language.

## Joint materials

Jointly approved pilot wording, diagrams, examples, and conclusions should be used only within the approved scope. Publication does not merge the two architectures or transfer ownership between the parties.

## No implied transfer or license

- Access does not transfer ownership.
- Interoperability does not imply assignment.
- Joint publication does not create a license to private implementations.
- Use of a name or mark does not imply endorsement beyond the approved pilot statement.
- No commercial reuse should be inferred from public availability.

## Approval requirement

NeoMundi should approve its public technical description, signal-field names and meanings, use of its name or marks, selected example, architecture diagram, joint conclusion, and commercial-contact language before public release.
