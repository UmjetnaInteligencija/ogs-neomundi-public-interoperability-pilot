# Joint Conclusion — Draft for Approval

> The NeoMundi × OGS interoperability pilot demonstrates a controlled transition from runtime observation to governance consequence. NeoMundi provides structured runtime signals concerning behavior, risk, variation, and drift. OGS evaluates what those signals may legitimately support, imposes review and revalidation requirements where necessary, prevents measurement from becoming self-authorizing execution authority, and preserves the result through governance receipts and replay.
>
> The current pilot is synthetic and architecture-level. Five corrected payload-bound receipts were generated, and an internal deterministic replay reproduced five documented classifications under the same payloads and explicit rules. No external action was authorized. The result is not independently reproduced, production-derived, safety-certified, or regulator-approved.
>
> The pilot's value lies in demonstrating how observability and admissibility can remain separate while operating through one traceable governance flow.
