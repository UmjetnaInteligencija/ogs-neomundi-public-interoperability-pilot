# Selected Public Example

## Scenario

A model service shows acceptable aggregate performance but increasing instability in a high-consequence workflow.

The NeoMundi-style signal indicates:

- rising response variance;
- inconsistent behavior across comparable requests;
- increased sensitivity to contextual phrasing;
- material uncertainty in causal attribution;
- no confirmed unsafe execution;
- insufficient evidence for a public reliability claim.

## Admissibility evaluation

The signal is useful for internal monitoring and technical review but does not support an unqualified public assurance, automatic rollback, regulatory-compliance assertion, or autonomous action.

## Outcome

```text
NeoMundi-style observation:
MATERIAL_BEHAVIORAL_DRIFT

OGS:
LIMITED_USE
REVIEW_REQUIRED
REVALIDATION_REQUIRED
NO_BIND
```

## Why it matters

The example preserves runtime visibility while preventing a risk or drift score from silently acquiring authority. It shows that the permitted consequence depends not only on the measurement but also on evidence burden, intended use, uncertainty, authority, scope, and consequence.
