# Public Claim Boundary

## Canonical formulation

> NeoMundi provides runtime observation, risk, and drift intelligence. OGS determines the admissible reliance, governance consequence, and permitted downstream use of those signals. The pilot demonstrates how observability can be converted into bounded, reviewable, and replayable governance objects without allowing measurement alone to become execution authority.

## Short formulation

> NeoMundi observes. OGS adjudicates what the observation may become.

## Controlled result formulation

> Five synthetic NeoMundi-style observation payloads were processed through an explicit OGS admissibility mapping. Five corrected payload-bound receipts were generated, and an internal deterministic replay reproduced all five documented classifications under the same payloads and explicit rules. No external action was authorized. The result is synthetic, internally verified, and not independently reproduced or production-derived.

## Permitted claims

The public release may state that:

- NeoMundi and OGS operate as distinct interoperable layers in the pilot design;
- NeoMundi supplies structured runtime observation, risk, variation, or drift signals;
- OGS evaluates permitted reliance and downstream governance consequence;
- measurement does not automatically create execution authority;
- five synthetic payloads were processed under an explicit mapping;
- five corrected payload-bound receipts were generated;
- internal same-payload, same-explicit-rules replay reproduced five documented classifications;
- no external action was authorized in the tested synthetic cases;
- the work is a proof of method, not a production deployment.

## Required qualifiers

Use these words where applicable:

- `synthetic`;
- `internally verified`;
- `internal deterministic replay`;
- `same payloads and explicit rules`;
- `not independently reproduced`;
- `not production-derived`;
- `no external action authorized in the tested cases`.

## Prohibited claims

Do not state or imply that:

- NeoMundi and OGS are production-integrated;
- the pilot proves real-world safety;
- the pilot is regulator-approved or safety-certified;
- live customer data was used;
- autonomous remediation was demonstrated;
- NeoMundi signals are inherently true;
- OGS independently validates the underlying measurements;
- the pilot proves universal enterprise applicability;
- the pilot eliminates human review;
- receipts are malicious-insider-resistant, HSM/KMS-backed, externally anchored, or non-repudiable;
- the replay was independently reproduced;
- the tested result establishes superiority over other architectures.

## Interpretation discipline

A public reader should be able to distinguish clearly among:

1. architectural design;
2. illustrative public examples;
3. internally verified synthetic evidence;
4. independent reproduction;
5. production evidence.

Only the first three are present here.
