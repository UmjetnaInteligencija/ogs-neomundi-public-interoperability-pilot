# Public Disclosure Boundary

## Public-safe material included

- high-level system boundaries;
- non-collapse doctrine;
- public interface fields;
- illustrative signal, governance, receipt, and replay objects;
- evidence classification and controlled claim;
- limitations, ownership, attribution, and publication controls;
- local integrity-verification tooling.

## Material deliberately excluded

- private prompts and semantic lenses;
- internal Petunia, DPA, Injector, CAOM, Judicator, or TAG-HAS decision logic;
- private taxonomies, thresholds, scoring rules, release rules, or policy tables;
- proprietary workflow maps, registries, authority models, or escalation logic;
- private source code or production infrastructure;
- customer data or partner-confidential information;
- private correspondence;
- patent-sensitive claim charts or counsel materials;
- unapproved logos and brand assets.

## Disclosure rule

A public description may identify component responsibilities and object boundaries without disclosing the private mechanisms that instantiate them.

## Primitive protection

The repository demonstrates separation, provenance, bounded reliance, receipts, and replay at the interface level. It does not disclose the internal primitives required to reproduce the full Petunia × Judicator architecture or NeoMundi implementation.
